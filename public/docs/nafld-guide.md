# Nonalcoholic Fatty Liver Disease Guide

## What it is
Nonalcoholic fatty liver disease (NAFLD/MASLD terminology in evolving use) is excess hepatic fat not primarily due to alcohol.

## Risk context
- Obesity
- Type 2 diabetes
- Dyslipidemia
- Metabolic syndrome

## Treatment approach
- Weight-loss centered lifestyle intervention
- Cardiometabolic risk factor optimization
- Specialist referral based on fibrosis risk stratification

## Non-pharmacologic care
- Calorie-deficit nutrition plan with sustainable adherence
- Physical activity progression
- Alcohol moderation counseling

## Monitoring and follow-up
- Liver enzyme trends, fibrosis risk scores, and metabolic marker control

## Guideline references
- AASLD and international liver society NAFLD/MASLD guidance
