# Chronic Constipation Guide

## What it is
Chronic constipation is infrequent or difficult bowel movements with associated symptom burden over time.

## First-line strategy
- Fiber optimization and hydration
- Osmotic or stimulant laxative protocol titration

## Escalation strategy
- Evaluate alarm features and secondary causes
- Consider secretagogues or prokinetic pathways if first-line therapy fails

## Non-pharmacologic care
- Daily bowel routine timing
- Physical activity support
- Pelvic floor dysfunction evaluation when indicated

## Monitoring and follow-up
- Stool frequency/consistency, straining burden, quality-of-life impact, and adverse effects
