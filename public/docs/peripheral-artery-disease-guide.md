# Peripheral artery disease guide

## Snapshot
- **Typical symptoms:** Exertional calf/thigh pain, reduced walking distance, delayed wound healing.
- **High-risk profile:** Diabetes, tobacco use, CKD, hypertension, hyperlipidemia.

## Initial workup
- Resting **ABI** for suspected PAD.
- Pulse exam, skin/foot exam, ulcer assessment.
- Consider vascular referral for severe symptoms, rest pain, or tissue loss.

## Treatment priorities
1. **Antiplatelet therapy** when indicated.
2. **High-intensity statin** unless contraindicated.
3. Optimize BP, glucose, and tobacco cessation.
4. Structured walking program and supervised exercise when available.

## Escalation triggers
- Rest pain, non-healing ulcers, gangrene, rapidly declining function.
- ABI decline with worsening symptoms.

## Follow-up cadence
- Reassess walking tolerance, wound status, med adherence, and smoking status every 4–12 weeks during active management.
