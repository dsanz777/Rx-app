# Smoking Cessation Guide

## Why it matters
Stopping tobacco use rapidly lowers cardiovascular, pulmonary, and oncologic risk.

## Evidence-based medications
- Nicotine replacement therapy (single or combination)
- Varenicline pathways
- Bupropion pathways when appropriate

## Behavioral support
- Structured quit plan and trigger mapping
- Follow-up touchpoints in first 2-4 weeks
- Relapse management as part of care, not failure

## Monitoring and follow-up
- Craving control, withdrawal burden, medication adverse effects, quit-day adherence

## Guideline references
- USPSTF tobacco cessation recommendation
- U.S. Public Health Service cessation framework
