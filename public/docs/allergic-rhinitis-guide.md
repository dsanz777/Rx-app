# Allergic rhinitis guide

## Snapshot
- **Common symptoms:** Sneezing, nasal congestion, rhinorrhea, postnasal drip, itchy eyes/nose.
- **Goal:** Symptom control with minimal sedation and improved daytime functioning.

## First-line strategy
1. Trigger reduction (dust, pollen, pet dander as feasible).
2. **Intranasal corticosteroid** daily during active symptom periods.
3. Add non-sedating oral antihistamine as needed.

## Step-up options
- Intranasal antihistamine for breakthrough symptoms.
- Combination intranasal steroid + antihistamine for persistent moderate-severe symptoms.
- Saline irrigation for mucus burden and comfort.

## Safety notes
- Review sedating antihistamine burden, especially in older adults.
- Reassess proper spray technique before escalating medication.

## Follow-up
- Recheck in 2–6 weeks after regimen changes.
- Consider allergy/immunology referral for refractory disease or unclear triggers.
