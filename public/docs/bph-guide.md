# Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia Guide

## What it is
Benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH) is nonmalignant enlargement of the prostate associated with lower urinary tract symptoms.

## Common symptoms
- Urinary hesitancy
- Weak stream
- Nocturia
- Incomplete emptying

## Treatment approach
- Mild symptoms: shared decision, watchful waiting, lifestyle steps.
- Moderate/severe symptoms: alpha-1 blockers and/or 5-alpha reductase inhibitor pathways based on prostate profile.

## Non-pharmacologic care
- Timed voiding and evening fluid moderation
- Caffeine/alcohol symptom-trigger review

## Monitoring and follow-up
- Symptom score trajectory, orthostatic effects, and urinary retention red flags
